## About Me

I am a technology and automation professional with 7+ years of experience across banking automation, robotic process automation, web development, and systems management. I currently lead RPA Development at BRAC Bank PLC, where I automate manual and repetitive processes, lead and mentor a lean team of 4 RPA developers, and work closely with business stakeholders. I designed and deployed the bank's first AI agent integrated with RPA for end-to-end process automation. My team builds every automation in-house in Python, using Selenium and PyAutoGUI for browser and desktop automation, EasyOCR, PaddleOCR, and Tesseract for document extraction, OpenCV and YOLO for image detection, and locally deployed LLMs (Qwen, GLM) for AI agents. The result is 55+ automations across 12 business areas, with 43 bots in live production saving 300+ man-hours of manual work every day, all on-premise and fully compliant.

I began my banking career in 2022 as a Young Leader (Management Trainee Officer) and previously worked as a Web Developer and IT Officer. I hold a BSc in Computer Science & Engineering from Ahsanullah University of Science and Technology, Bangladesh.
